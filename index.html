How a verdict is reached

Each food's average day score on the days you ate it gets compared against the days you didn't. It needs at least three days on each side before it will say anything. Until then it says Watching rather than guessing.

Why it sometimes refuses

If two foods always turn up together, dal and roti for instance, no amount of data can separate them. Both get marked Tangled. Eat one without the other and they come apart. A food you eat nearly every day gets No gap, because there is no comparison to make until you take a few days off it.

Tolerance per channel

Every channel starts untested, sitting at the published cutoff. Run a challenge and replace the guess with your own number.

How a challenge runs

Three escalating doses, then three or four days back at baseline before the next channel.

Fructans and GOS need a rest day between doses. They can produce symptoms for up to 48 hours, so testing them back to back smears one day's reaction onto the next. Lactose, fructose and polyols clear faster and can run on consecutive days.

Is food even your problem?
Why this question comes first

For a large share of people with IBS the gut produces an ordinary amount of gas and the nervous system amplifies the signal. Diet does very little for that, and restricting further does harm. So before you delete food from your life, this checks whether your discomfort actually follows what you ate. If there isn't enough to go on, it says so instead of inventing an answer.

Rate, not total

Different FODMAPs land in different watches. Polyols and excess fructose pull water into the small bowel and hit within 1 to 3 hours. Lactose ferments early, at 2 to 4 hours. Fructans and GOS ferment further down in the colon, so they arrive late, at 4 to 8 hours.

So the same plate in a different watch makes for a different day. Eat the rajma in the early night, Pradosh, from nine onwards, and the peak lands in the deep night while you are asleep.

One dish is not one number

Restaurant gravies are thickened with cashew paste and cream, while the Jain version drops onion and garlic entirely. Same name on the menu, three different foods. So every curry here carries all three and you pick the one you are actually eating.

Soaking works because GOS dissolves in water. Soak pulses, throw away the water and rinse them, and α-GOS falls by 10 to 40 percent. That is why rajma has a soaking setting rather than a red light.

What a unit means

Load is counted in serve-units, which is grams divided by the published per-serving cutoff. So 0.30 g of fructan comes to exactly 1.00 unit, and one unit is one "low" serving. The curves are added up across every meal and checked against your own thresholds.
